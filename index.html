A bit about me

I'm a backend-focused full-stack developer with 5+ years building scalable Node.js services and the systems that connect them to the outside world. Most of my work lives at the messy edge where a product meets a third-party platform — where webhooks arrive late, tokens expire, and an API returns something the docs never mentioned.

Lately that's meant a lot of AI in production: getting a support agent to answer from a knowledge base it can be trusted on, and knowing when to hand a customer to a human instead. I care about the unglamorous parts — retries, rate limits, timezones, and the difference between a reply that's confident and one that's correct.

I work day to day across Node.js, NestJS, React and Next.js, with MongoDB and PostgreSQL on AWS.
